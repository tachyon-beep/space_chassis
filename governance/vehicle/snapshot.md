## What the vehicle is

**Space Aurora is one program building one product** — a fleet of self-modifying agents and the
spacecraft they fly — built by two project teams of equal importance working in parallel, and
coordinated by Emmy (John, 2026-10-10; `../README.md`). The vehicle is one of the two teams; governance
calls each team a *line*. The work divides, never the product: every task has one lead team, which sets
the rule, and where the other team does the same thing on its side it follows that rule
(`../coordination.md`).

What this team builds is the mission simulator on the far side of the window: one Apollo-style CSM/LM stack flown by a fleet
of agents through per-agent directories under `/diode/<slug>/`, and nothing else. It is its own
repository, `tachyon-beep/space_vehicle`, vendored into the chassis as the git submodule
`docs/deep_research/vehicle/` and run from its own image, `space-chassis-vehicle`, at `/opt/vehicle`; no
agent image carries any of it (chassis plan 8, John 2026-10-10). The two teams' parts of the product
meet at the window contract, `docs/diode-contract.md` — which the vehicle team owns and leads, and whose
changes take effect when the chassis adopts them — and at the vehicle service's deployment, which the
chassis team owns and leads (see `interface.md`; the chassis side is `../chassis/interface.md`).

- Configuration is data: `vehicle.yaml`, `mission.yaml`, `channels.yaml`, `coupling.yaml`,
  `presentation.yaml`, and eleven domains under `domains/*/` — avionics, comms, consumables, crew,
  eclss, gnc, power, propulsion, rcs, structure, thermal — each with its commands, components,
  points, profiles and fault policy. `plant.md` is the physics contract (`step()`, integrator classes, ordering,
  determinism, the truth/evidence boundary).
- Tools: `tools/console.py` (the executive and the window), `tools/plant.py` (the plant, readiness,
  build order), `tools/checkpoint.py` (checkpoint format), `tools/faults.py`, `tools/check_vehicle.py`
  (the linter/referee of the configuration), `tools/generate_help.py`, `tools/generate_readme.py`,
  `tools/measure_clock.py`.
- Tests: `tests/test_vehicle_config.py` — the referee, one file.
